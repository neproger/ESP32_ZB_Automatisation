## Implications

- Universal compatibility is realistic **because ZHC is already compositional**:
  port the shared mechanisms once and the definitions become data.
- One-off converters/configure closures are 17.5% of definitions but ~2–3% of
  uses. They should be an explicit unsupported/`PARTIAL` tail, not hand-ported
  model by model.
- Configure has more one-off closures than converters (602), often option
  closures. The compiler must parameterise shared configure generators instead
  of copying each device's closure.
- Priority for the Rust side: `on_off`, level/brightness, color/color-temp,
  power-on behavior, ignore-transition handling; then a parameterised
  `reporting.*` configure generator. Lights are the largest cluster, not an edge
  case.
- The catalog inventory's `full = 0` measured the current Rust table, not the
  structure of ZHC. This behavior inventory is the better basis for estimating
  work: **~82% of definitions are compositions of shared functions**.
